Privacy Policy

Draft — last updated August 2026

Draft for review. This is a working draft, not a final legal document. Because Elev8 collects health information, this page needs sign-off from a healthcare attorney before the site goes live — particularly the sections on medical records and data sharing, which should be checked against HIPAA and applicable state law.

What we collect

When you contact Elev8, book a consult, or become a member, we may collect your name, contact information, and — with your consent, once you're a client — health information relevant to your care, gathered during intake and ongoing treatment.

How we use it

  • To respond to consult requests and schedule appointments
  • To provide medical care, including coordination with labs, specialists, and other providers you authorize
  • To communicate with you about your membership and care
  • To meet legal and recordkeeping obligations required of a medical practice

How we protect it

Health information you share as a client is treated as confidential medical record information, handled under the same standards of discretion Elev8 applies to every part of the client relationship. [Final language on safeguards, encryption, and retention to be confirmed with counsel.]

What we don't do

We do not sell client information. We do not share your health information with third parties except as needed to provide your care, coordinate with providers you've authorized, or as required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting concierge@elev8md.co. [Specific rights under HIPAA and applicable state privacy law to be added by counsel.]
