The Elev8 Method

Six lenses. One judgment.

Nothing is recommended until it's been weighed from every angle.

Evidence

What does the best available research tell us?

Experience

What does accumulated clinical experience suggest?

Biology

Is the mechanism physiologically plausible?

Individual

What's distinctive about the person in front of us?

Values

What do you want from your life and care?

Risk

What happens if we act, wait, or do nothing?

Synthesis: Judgment.

Excellent medicine requires knowledge, discipline, and the confidence to say: “You don't need this.”
